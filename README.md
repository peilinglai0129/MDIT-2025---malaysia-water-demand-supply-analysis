# Every Drop Counts: Evaluating Water Demand, Supply, and Efficiency in Malaysia
### MDIT x DOSM Datathon 2025 | Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview

This project analyzes water demand, supply, and efficiency across Malaysian states from 2003 to 2022. It uses publicly available data to examine water consumption patterns, resource efficiency, and supply sustainability.

## Project Objectives

1. To analyse state-level water consumption in Malaysia, with particular emphasis on distinguishing between domestic and non-domestic water use.
2. To assess water demand efficiency across states by calculating Liters Per Capita per Day (LPCD) and determining whether highly populated states consume water more or less efficiently compared to less populated states.
3. To evaluate the efficiency and sustainability of state-level water supply systems by examining the interrelationship between water production, Non-Revenue Water (NRW), and the Water Stress Index.
4. To compare water consumption with water production across states, identifying regions where demand exceeds supply (water-stressed areas) and those with underutilised resources.

## Step-by-Step Instructions to Open and Navigate the Dashboard

- Navigate the dashboard 
Click the next page in the Power BI window to switch between different pages
To return to the previous page of the dashboard, click the left arrow button located at the bottom left of the dashboard.
Maximize the window to see all visual elements clearly.

- Objective 1 :
In the first graph (Water Consumption by State and Year), select a specific year and state by clicking on the chart.
The selected state’s water consumption value will be displayed.
Once a state and year are selected, the other visuals will update automatically.
The Domestic vs Non-Domestic consumption chart will adjust to show the sectoral breakdown.
The map visualization will highlight the chosen state.
The dashboard will also display the average LPCD (Liters Per Capita per Day), the average Non-Revenue Water (NRW) percentage, and the average Stress Index for the selected filters.

- Objective 2 :
Use Year filter that placed on the left side of the dashboard to select specific year.
For interactive visuals, press the play button to see changes over time (2003–2022).
Click on the speech bubble at the bottom right to view the article titled 'Status of Water Quality Subject to Sand Mining in the Kelantan River, Kelantan'.

- Objective 3 :
Select a specific state by clicking on the bar and line chart.
Once a state is selected, the other visuals will update automatically.
In the first graph, the dashboard will display Water Production values and Non-Revenue Water (NRW) percentages for each year from 2003 to 2022.
In the second graph, the dashboard will display average NRW percent, average stress index and sum of water production value for a specific state. 

- Objective 4 : 
Use Year filter that placed on the left side of the dashboard to select specific year.
For interactive visuals, press the play button to see changes over time (2003–2022).
To view the water gap of the states for each year, use the Year filter placed on the left side of the dashboard.
Select a specific year, the dashboard will update to show the gap between water consumption and water production for all states in that year.


## Additional Requirements

## Notes, Limitations & Considerations

- Data is available only from 2003 to 2022.
- Calculated fields (e.g., LPCD, Stress Index, NRW%) are based on assumptions from available formulas. 
- Dashboard works best on Power BI Desktop; some navigation buttons may not function in the web version.
- All analysis is for academic purposes and may not reflect official government assessments.
